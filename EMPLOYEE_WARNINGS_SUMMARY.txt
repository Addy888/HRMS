================================================================================
EMPLOYEE RESPONSE FLOW (EXISTING)
================================================================================

1. HR creates warning
2. HR issues warning (status: ISSUED)
3. Notification sent to employee
4. Employee opens /employee/hr-actions
5. Warning appears in list
6. Employee clicks warning
7. Detail page shows (status auto-updates to VIEWED)
8. Employee reads warning
9. Employee acknowledges (if needed)
10. IF responseRequired:
    a. Employee sees response textarea
    b. Employee types response
    c. Employee clicks "Submit Response"
    d. Response saved to database (HRAction.responseText)
    e. Status updates to RESPONSE_SUBMITTED
    f. Notification sent to HR
    g. Response becomes read-only
11. HR opens warning detail
12. HR sees "Employee Response" section
13. HR reads employee's response
14. HR resolves or takes further action
